# Education Module User Guide

## Introduction

Welcome to the Education Module! This comprehensive learning platform helps students, parents, tutors, and institutes manage tuition, skill courses, scholarships, and educational support in one place.

---

## Getting Started

### 1. Sign Up / Login

First, create an account or log in to access the Education Module:

1. Navigate to the Education section from the main menu
2. If not logged in, you'll be redirected to the login page
3. Enter your credentials or sign up for a new account

### 2. Set Up Your Profile

After logging in:

1. Click on **"360 Dashboard"** from the navigation menu
2. Fill in your Role Profile:
   - **Primary Role**: Student, Parent, Tutor, or Institute Admin
   - **Student Name**: Your name or your child's name
   - **Class Level**: Current class (e.g., Class 10, Plus Two)
   - **Target Exam**: Your target exam (e.g., SSLC, NEET, JEE)
   - **Preferred Language**: English, Malayalam, or Hindi
   - **Career Goal**: Your career aspiration
3. Click **"Save Role Profile"**

## Browsing and Enrolling in Courses

### Browse Courses

1. Click **"Courses"** from the navigation menu
2. Use the search bar to find courses by:
   - Course name
   - Level (Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced)
   - Topic or keyword
3. View course cards showing:
   - Course title
   - Level and duration
   - Price (Free or paid amount)
   - Course value score
   - Description

### View Course Details

1. Click **"View Details"** on any course card
2. Review:
   - Full course description
   - Module and lesson breakdown (syllabus)
   - Learning outcomes
   - Duration and level
   - Certificate availability
3. Click **"Back to Courses"** to return

### Enroll in a Course

#### For Free Courses:

1. Click **"Enroll Now"** on a free course
2. You'll be instantly enrolled
3. The course appears in **"My Learning"**

#### For Paid Courses:

1. Click **"Enroll Now"** on a paid course
2. Review the payment amount
3. A Razorpay payment window will open
4. Complete payment using:
   - UPI
   - Credit/Debit Card
   - Net Banking
5. After successful payment, you'll be enrolled
6. Check your email for payment confirmation

### Track Your Progress

1. Go to **"My Learning"** from the navigation menu
2. View all your enrolled courses with progress bars
3. Update progress:
   - Click **"+10% Lesson Done"** after completing a lesson
   - Click **"-5% Revisit"** if you need to review
4. Click **"Continue Learning"** to resume a course

## Booking Tuition

### Request Tuition

1. From the **Home** page, scroll to **"Book Subject Tuition"**
2. Fill in the details:
   - **Subject**: Select from Mathematics, Science, English, etc.
   - **Class Level**: Your current class
   - **Contact Phone**: Your phone number (optional but recommended)
3. Click **"Request Tuition"**
4. You'll see matched tutors with:
   - Name and experience
   - Rating and match score
   - Hourly fee

### Track Tuition Requests

1. Click **"Tuition Tracker"** from the navigation menu
2. View all your tuition requests with:
   - Subject and class level
   - Current status (submitted, matched, booked, etc.)
   - Assigned tutor details
   - Session history
3. Update status as sessions progress

### Manage Tuition Sessions

1. In **Tuition Tracker**, find your request
2. Click **"Add Session"** to schedule a new session:
   - Select date and time
   - Set duration (15-240 minutes)
   - Add agenda/topic
3. After each session, mark attendance:
   - **Attended**: Session completed
   - **Missed**: Student didn't attend
   - **Rescheduled**: Session moved to another time

## Managing Certificates

### Upload a Certificate

1. Click **"Certificates"** from the navigation menu
2. Fill in certificate details:
   - **Title**: Certificate name (required, min 3 characters)
   - **Issuer**: Organization that issued it
   - **Completed On**: Completion date
   - **Credential ID**: Verification ID if available
3. Optionally upload certificate file (JPEG, PNG, or PDF, max 5MB)
4. Click **"Upload Certificate"**

### Certificate Verification

Certificates have three statuses:
- **Uploaded**: Newly added, pending verification
- **Verified**: Confirmed by admin
- **Rejected**: Needs additional proof

To improve verification:
- Add credential ID
- Upload clear certificate image
- Provide issuer details

### Share Your Certificates

1. Go to **"Certificates"** section
2. View your wallet share text
3. Use it to share your achievements on social media or resumes

## Using the Study Path Builder

### Generate a Study Plan

1. Click **"Study Path"** from the navigation menu
2. Fill in your details:
   - **Class/Level**: Your current education level
   - **Learning Goal**: What you want to achieve
   - **Daily Study Hours**: How much time you can dedicate (1-8 hours)
   - **Preferred Language**: Language for study materials
3. Click to generate the plan
4. Review your weekly study schedule
5. Click **"Add to My Learning"** to save it

### Study Plan Example

For "Class 10 - SSLC high score":
- **Monday**: Revise theory for 2 hours and write short answers
- **Tuesday**: Practice previous-year questions
- **Wednesday**: Prepare one-page notes for difficult topics
- **Thursday**: Take a timed mini mock test
- **Friday**: Review mistakes and rework weak concepts
- **Saturday**: Full revision with formulas and definitions
- **Sunday**: Light recap and next-week planning

## Troubleshooting

### Course Enrollment Issues

**Problem**: "Already enrolled" error but course not in My Learning

**Solution**:
1. Refresh the page
2. Log out and log back in
3. Check **"My Learning"** again
4. Contact support if issue persists

---

### Payment Failed

**Problem**: Payment deducted but enrollment not confirmed

**Solution**:
1. Wait 5-10 minutes for confirmation
2. Check your email for payment confirmation
3. If still not enrolled, contact support with:
   - Transaction ID
   - Payment screenshot
   - Course name

---

### Certificate Upload Failed

**Problem**: "Upload failed" error

**Solution**:
1. Check file size (must be < 5MB)
2. Verify file type (JPEG, PNG, or PDF only)
3. Try uploading without file (add details only)
4. Retry with smaller file size

---

### Cannot See Updated Progress

**Problem**: Progress not updating after clicking +10%

**Solution**:
1. Check internet connection
2. Look for "Syncing" message
3. Refresh the page
4. Progress saved locally if offline; will sync when online

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q: Can I enroll in multiple courses?**
A: Yes, there's no limit on course enrollments.

**Q: Are certificates recognized?**
A: Certificates from recognized issuers are verified and can be used for job applications.

**Q: Can I get a refund?**
A: Refund policy depends on course provider. Check course details.

**Q: How do I cancel a tuition request?**
A: Go to Tuition Tracker, find your request, click "Cancel Request".

**Q: Is my payment information secure?**
A: Yes, we use Razorpay's secure payment gateway. We never store your card details.

**Q: Can I use the module offline?**
A: Limited features work offline. Your progress saves locally and syncs when you're back online.

**Q: How often should I update my progress?**
A: Update after each study session for accurate tracking.

**Q: What if I forgot my password?**
A: Use the "Forgot Password" link on the login page.
