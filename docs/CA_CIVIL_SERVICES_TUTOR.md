## 📚 CA (Chartered Accountancy) Preparation

### Levels Covered

#### 1. CA Foundation
**Subjects:**
- Principles and Practice of Accounting
- Business Laws and Business Correspondence
- Business Mathematics and Statistics
- Business Economics

**Topics Include:**
- Accounting fundamentals (Journal, Ledger, Trial Balance)
- Golden rules of accounting
- Final accounts preparation
- Partnership accounting
- Indian Contract Act 1872
- Company Law basics
- Sets, Matrices, Calculus
- Statistical measures
- Demand and supply
- Market structures

#### 2. CA Intermediate
**Subjects:**
- Advanced Accounting
- Corporate and Other Laws
- Income Tax
- Cost and Management Accounting
- Auditing and Assurance

**Topics Include:**
- Partnership accounts (admission, retirement, dissolution)
- Company accounts (Share capital, debentures)
- Accounting Standards (AS)
- Companies Act 2013
- Income tax provisions
- Previous year, Assessment year
- Salary, House property income
- Capital gains
- Cost sheet, Material costing
- Labour costing, Overhead allocation
- Standard costing, Budgeting
- Audit planning and evidence
- Standards on Auditing (SA)

#### 3. CA Final
**Subjects:**
- Financial Reporting
- Strategic Financial Management
- Advanced Auditing
- Direct Tax Laws
- Indirect Tax Laws (GST)

**Topics Include:**
- Ind AS (Indian Accounting Standards)
- IFRS convergence
- Consolidated financial statements
- Cost of capital (CAPM, WACC)
- Capital budgeting
- Mergers and acquisitions
- CARO (Companies Auditor's Report Order)
- Peer review
- Corporate taxation (MAT, AMT)
- Transfer pricing
- GST (CGST, SGST, IGST)
- GST returns and compliance
