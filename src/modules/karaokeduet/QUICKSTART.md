# 🚀 Quick Start Guide - Karaoke Duet Pro

Get started with your first karaoke duet in 5 minutes!

## Step 1: Create a Session (30 seconds)

1. Open Karaoke Duet Pro
2. Click **"Create New Duet Session"**
3. Fill in:
   - **Session Title:** "My First Duet"
   - **Song Title:** e.g., "Perfect"
   - **Artist:** e.g., "Ed Sheeran"
   - **BPM:** 95 (or leave default 120)
4. Click **"Create Session"**

✅ **Session created!**

---

## Step 2: Get Lyrics (Optional - 15 seconds)

1. In the session view, find the **"Fetch Lyrics"** card
2. Song title and artist should be pre-filled
3. Click **"🔍 Fetch Free Lyrics"**
4. Wait 2-5 seconds

✅ **Lyrics loaded and synced!**

💡 *If lyrics not found, you can paste them manually or skip this step*

---

## Step 3: Record Singer A (1-3 minutes)

1. Click **"🎙️ Start Recording Duet"**
2. Keep **"Singer A"** selected
3. Click the big red **⏺️ record button**
4. Allow microphone access if prompted
5. Sing your part!
6. Click **"⏹️ Stop"** when done

✅ **Singer A recorded!**

---

## Step 4: Record Singer B (1-3 minutes)

1. Click **"🎤 Singer B"** button
2. Click the big red **⏺️ record button**
3. Sing the second part!
4. Click **"⏹️ Stop"** when done

✅ **Singer B recorded!**

---

## Step 5: Mix & Download (30 seconds)

1. Scroll down to **"Final Mix"** section
2. You should see "✅ 2/2 Recordings"
3. Click **"🎵 Mix & Download Final Duet"**
4. Wait 10-30 seconds for mixing
5. Your mixed duet downloads automatically!

✅ **Done! Your duet is ready!** 🎉

---

## 🎯 Pro Tips

### Better Recording Quality
- Use headphones (prevents echo)
- Record in quiet environment
- Keep mic 6-12 inches away
- Do a test recording first

### If You Make a Mistake
- Click **"🗑️ Delete & Re-record"** to redo a singer
- Or just create a new session

### Save Your Work
- Everything saves automatically to your device
- No internet needed after lyrics are fetched
- Access your sessions anytime from the home screen

---

## 🆘 Quick Troubleshooting

**Can't hear my recording?**
- Check browser volume
- Check system volume
- Try playing in different browser

**Microphone not working?**
- Click the 🔒 in address bar
- Allow microphone permission
- Refresh the page

**Mixing taking forever?**
- Normal for first time (30-60 seconds)
- Don't close browser during mixing
- Make sure recordings aren't too long
