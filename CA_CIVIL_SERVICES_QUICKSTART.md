## 💯 Scoring Strategy

### For CA Students

**Foundation (70% to Pass)**
- Accounting: Practice 50+ problems
- Laws: Understand, don't cram
- Math: Formula mastery
- Economics: Diagrams important

**Intermediate (40% to Pass per group)**
- Advanced Accounting: Conceptual clarity
- Income Tax: Practical examples
- Cost Accounting: Format practice
- Auditing: SA understanding

**Final (40% to Pass per group)**
- Ind AS: IFRS convergence
- SFM: Calculator speed
- Tax Laws: Recent amendments
- GST: Return filing practice

### For UPSC Prelims (100 marks to qualify)

**Score Distribution:**
- Polity: 15-20 questions → Target: 75%
- Economy: 12-15 questions → Target: 70%
- Geography: 12-15 questions → Target: 70%
- History: 15-20 questions → Target: 65%
- Current Affairs: 15-20 questions → Target: 80%
- Science: 10-12 questions → Target: 60%

**Strategy:**
- Attempt 85-90 questions confidently
- Negative marking: -0.33 per wrong answer
- Target: 95-105 marks (safe score)

### For UPSC Mains (1750 marks total)

**Answer Writing:**
- **10 marks:** 150 words, 15-18 minutes
- **15 marks:** 250 words, 22-25 minutes
- **20 marks:** 400 words, 30-35 minutes

**Essay (250 marks):**
- 1000-1200 words
- 3 hours time
- Structure: Intro → Body (3-4 themes) → Conclusion

## 🔥 Pro Tips

### CA Students

1. **Don't Skip Basics**
   - Foundation is crucial
   - Weak foundation = Difficult Intermediate

2. **Practice > Theory**
   - Solve 100+ problems per topic
   - Practical exposure during articleship

3. **Time Management in Exam**
   - 1.8 minutes per mark
   - Easy questions first
   - No negative marking → Attempt all

4. **Use Personal Tutor**
   - Daily 30-minute quiz
   - Weak area identification
   - Progressive difficulty

### UPSC Aspirants

1. **NCERT is Non-Negotiable**
   - Class 6-12 NCERTs
   - Multiple readings
   - Note-making

2. **Current Affairs Daily**
   - Newspaper (Hindu/Indian Express)
   - Monthly magazine
   - Personal Tutor quizzes

3. **Answer Writing from Day 1**
   - Prelims: MCQ practice
   - Mains: Descriptive from start
   - Personal Tutor evaluation

4. **Mock Tests Weekly**
   - Simulate exam conditions
   - Time management
   - Accuracy improvement
